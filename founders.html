Why this

Software ships in seconds. Hardware ships next week.

Between us we've lived the hardware loop from both ends — lab work at an RF company on one side, award-winning additive design and fabrication on the other — and we've built enough software to know how the other loop feels. Software is tight: change it, build it, see it, fix it — minutes. Hardware isn't. You send a board to a fab, wait out the week, find the one mistake, and start the clock over.

That gap — between how fast you can think and how slowly you can hold the result in your hand — is the thing we want to close. PrometheusPCB is our answer: pull fabrication onto the bench, so the wait stops being the bottleneck and your ideas start being the bottleneck again.

The founders

Two disciplines, one machine.

Before PrometheusPCB he worked in the lab at Stalker Radar, an RF company — hands-on with exactly the class of high-frequency hardware ceramic boards are made for. What's next

Early, and looking for the right people.

PrometheusPCB is early and moving fast. Texas Tech's Innovation Hub and an NSF I-Corps grant gave us the room to chase this now instead of someday — and with the paper research settled, we're heads-down building our first functional prototype and talking to the hardware teams and labs who'd use it.

The seat we're still filling is deep ceramic materials — ceramic processing, paste rheology, constrained sintering. If the co-fire shrinkage problem reads as a challenge rather than a dealbreaker, or you just want a ceramic fab on your bench someday, let's talk.
